01

Create your company in minutes

Open LedgerLocal and create a .ledger file for your business. You get a ready-made chart of accounts — bank, AR, AP, income, and expense categories — so you can start bookkeeping the same day.

  • One file per company; open multiple businesses from one app
  • Customize accounts or add bank accounts anytime
  • Set default tax rate and invoice payment terms in Settings
  • Upload a company logo for invoice PDFs

Invoice customers and get paid

Create professional invoices with line items, inventory products, and automatic tax. Export PDFs, email from your client, and record payments when money arrives.

  • Multi-line invoices with inventory items and sales prices
  • Default due dates (Net 15, Net 30, etc.) from company settings
  • Batch invoicing for multiple customers at once
  • Track partial payments and open balances

03

Import and categorize bank activity

Bring in transactions from CSV, OFX, or QIF exports. LedgerLocal suggests categories using your rules, fuzzy matching, and on-device learning — then you review and approve before anything posts.

  • Never auto-posts without your approval
  • Create rules: “description contains STARBUCKS → Meals”
  • Bulk approve or skip transactions
  • Reconcile against bank statements monthly

Run reports you can trust

Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, cash flow, and AR/AP aging — on cash or accrual basis. Drill into any account to see the transactions behind the numbers.

Own, back up, and share your data

Your entire business lives in one local file. Back it up to a USB drive or cloud folder you control. Send an encrypted package to your accountant without granting cloud access.

  • One-click backup and restore
  • Encrypted accountant export (.llx) — read-only for CPAs
  • Multi-company: switch files without reinstalling
  • Inventory tracking and payroll calculator (Pro tier)
